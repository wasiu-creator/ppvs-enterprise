🗳 PPVS Enterprise — Survey Guide

What is PPVS?

Under Nexus Global's Tier-1 Security Protocol, all workforce surveys must be cryptographically anonymous. No manager, HR officer, or system administrator may link a response to an individual employee. PPVS enforces this guarantee at the protocol level.

The Privacy-Preserving Voting System uses Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKP) to let employees prove they are enrolled and have not yet responded — without revealing who they are or which option they chose. All rules are enforced by a tamper-proof smart contract.

How to run a survey simulation

1
Connect. Start the Hardhat node and deploy the contract. Paste the contract address into the Connect panel and click Connect.
2
Enrol employees. Type employee names in the Voter Roster panel (e.g. Emma — London, Raj — Mumbai). Each employee receives a cryptographic identity generated locally in the browser — secrets never leave their device.
3
Register on-chain. Click Register All Voters On-Chain. Only each employee's commitment hash is sent to the blockchain — no name, location, or personal data.
4
Open the survey. Click Start Voting (HR admin action). The survey window is now open globally.
5
Cast preference votes. Select an employee and a work model (In-Person / Remote / Hybrid), then click Cast ZK Vote. A nullifier is revealed — proving the employee hasn't responded before, without linking back to their identity.
6
Test double-response prevention. Select the same employee and vote again. The chain rejects it with "double-vote detected" — the nullifier is already recorded on-chain.
7
Close & view results. Click End Voting then Refresh Results to see the aggregated workforce preference tally.

Security Guarantees (Nexus Tier-1 Protocol)

🔒 Employee Anonymity
Each employee's secret and nullifierSecret are generated and held only on their own device. The blockchain sees only the commitment hash — neither HR nor management can reverse it to identify the respondent.
🚫 No Double Responding
Each response reveals a unique nullifier = Poseidon(nullifierSecret). Once stored on-chain, any second submission with the same nullifier is rejected. The nullifier cannot be linked back to the employee's commitment.
✅ Response Validity
The circuit enforces that the respondent knows the secret behind their registered commitment AND that the selected option index is within range (0 = In-Person, 1 = Remote, 2 = Hybrid). Invalid proofs are rejected before any state change.
📊 Verifiable Tally
Anyone can call getResults() on the contract to verify the final count — no trusted third-party tallier needed. Results are transparent and tamper-proof.

Cryptographic Identity (per employee)

secret — random 31-byte value, never leaves the employee's device
nullifierSecret — random 31-byte value, never leaves the employee's device
commitment = Poseidon(secret, nullifierSecret) — enrolled on-chain by HR
nullifier = Poseidon(nullifierSecret) — revealed once when casting the vote
